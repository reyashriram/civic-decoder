    03 / KNOW THE STRUCTURE

    Who does
    what?

    Mumbai's civic life involves different levels of government. Knowing which institution handles what is a useful first step.

    01

    Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC / MCGM)

    Mumbai's municipal corporation. Its civic responsibilities include services such as roads, solid waste management, drainage, water supply and public health functions.

    CITY LEVEL
    02

    Ward offices

    BMC administers the city through 24 administrative wards. Your ward can matter when identifying the local office responsible for a civic issue.

    LOCAL ADMIN
    03

    Government of Maharashtra

    The state government makes and administers decisions across areas such as state policy, policing, transport and public administration. Responsibilities can overlap with municipal services.

    STATE LEVEL
    i

    Important: Responsibility can depend on the exact location and issue. A road, utility or transport service may not always be managed by the same authority. Check the official source before filing.
